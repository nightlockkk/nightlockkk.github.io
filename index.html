I'm a third-year undergraduate studying Computer Science at the University of Washington, where I do research on multi-agent RL and exploration in the SocialRL Lab. Broadly, I'm interested in what models represent internally, how new capabilities emerge, and how learning systems can develop the capacity for introspection and recursive self-improvement.

My interest in intelligence, pattern recognition, consciousness, and the optimal usage of knowledge predates my interest in AI and machines. Naturally, from the brain :)

Previously, I was an MLE intern at d-Matrix, where I developed the Spec2Sim pipeline.

Outside of academics, I like hiking, enjoying windy days (when presented), philosophical discussions, and formalizing my relatively absurd visions for the world.
